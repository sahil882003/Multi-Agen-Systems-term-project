Explain STL results

G means always; F means eventually. t is the current simulation tick; D is each order's deadline.

Positive margin: satisfied. Zero margin: satisfied exactly at the limit. Negative margin: violated. Safety is sampled at ticks; swept collisions are checked separately.

Battery margin uses percentage points: 13.1% charge minus a 12% reserve is +1.1 points.

Deadline categories do not overlap: on time, delivered late, overdue undelivered, and pending. Total violations = late + overdue undelivered. Pending means not delivered and deadline not yet passed; it is not a success. Delivery exactly at D is on time.

Rejected is a planning outcome, separate from deadline status. Ending a run does not satisfy pending obligations. New violations are recorded once in the event log.
